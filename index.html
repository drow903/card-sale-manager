GETTING STARTED

From first claim to final shipment.

Follow the workflow below, reopen the guided walkthrough at any time, or create an anonymous support report if something goes wrong.

1

Import and prepare

Import a spreadsheet, review its column mapping, match images, and copy listings one at a time.

2

Record claims and offers

Assign buyers in Claims Desk. Review, counter, accept, or reject offers before they enter an order.

3

Confirm totals and payment

Choose shipping, verify addresses, copy buyer summaries, and mark payments without exposing purchase costs.

4

Pack and ship

Check every card, print packing slips or PWE labels, add tracking, and copy the shipping message.

Quick reference

Important rules and where your information lives.

Your data stays local

Sales are stored on this computer. The green save indicator confirms when changes are safely written.

Backups are automatic

A rotating daily backup is kept locally, with additional protection before app updates.

Shipping rule

Orders over 3 cards or $50 default to PMWT. You can manually override either shipping choice.

Need support?

The diagnostic report contains app health and counts only—never buyer names, addresses, card names, or image paths.
